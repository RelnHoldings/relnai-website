Features
Everything a
sideline parent
needs.

StatTap puts a full stat-tracking system in your pocket. Tap stats in real time, earn achievements for showing up, and build a season-by-season record of your athlete's journey.

01
Live Game Tracking

Tap stats in real time from the sideline. A bingo-board interface makes it fast and intuitive — no coaching degree required.

02
Multi-Sport Support

Volleyball, basketball, soccer, baseball, softball, and football. One app, every sport your kid plays.

03
Achievement System

Parents earn XP and achievements for tracking games. Stone through Diamond tiers — a full progression system for the most dedicated sideline parents.

04
Trophy Case

Every achievement earns a trophy that upgrades over time. Your trophy case is a visual record of every season you showed up for.

05
Season & Tournament Tracking

Organize games into tournaments and seasons. View career totals, season averages, and per-game breakdowns across all sports.

06
Kids View

Each player gets a unique code. They can view their own stats in a read-only kids mode — no account needed, no data entered by children.

Six Sports
One app for
every season.

StatTap supports six sports out of the box — each with its own stat set, scoring system, and period structure. Add sports to a player profile and switch between them on the home screen.
