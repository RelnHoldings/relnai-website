Section 01

Introduction

Welcome to StatTap ("the App"), a youth sports stat-tracking application operated by Reln Holdings LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), an Ohio limited liability company. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users and the children whose information is managed through the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and your rights regarding that information. By using StatTap, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Questions or concerns? Contact us at privacy@relnai.com.

Section 02

Who Uses StatTap

StatTap is designed for use by parents and legal guardians ("parents") who track athletic performance data on behalf of their minor children ("players"). Children do not create accounts or register directly.

Children may access a limited, read-only "Kids View" of their own stats using a unique player code generated by their parent. StatTap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts or provide personal information directly to us.

Section 03

Information We Collect

Parent Account

When you create a parent account, we collect:

  • ◆Full name
  • ◆Email address
  • ◆Password (stored in encrypted form via Supabase Auth)
  • ◆Subscription tier (free or premium)

Player Profiles

When you create a player profile for your child, you may provide:

  • ◆Player name
  • ◆Date of birth (used to calculate age only)
  • ◆A system-generated unique player code for Kids View access
  • ◆Sport-specific data: jersey number, team name, primary position, preferred stat selections — stored per sport

Game and Performance Data

When you record games, we collect game date, opponent name, sport, period/set information, team scores, in-game stat events and point values, and season and tournament associations.

Achievement and Points Data

The App tracks parent engagement through a gamified achievement system, including XP earned, current tier (Stone through Diamond), and achievements earned with their trophy levels.

Automatically Collected Information

We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or precise location data. Standard server logs maintained by Supabase may capture IP addresses and request metadata for security and debugging purposes.

Section 04

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to create and manage your account, provide stat-tracking and achievement features, enable the Kids View, process subscriptions, send transactional communications, improve the App, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use children's data for advertising, behavioral profiling, or any purpose other than providing the stat-tracking features you have set up on their behalf.

Section 05

Children's Privacy — COPPA Compliance

StatTap is designed with children's privacy as a priority and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Children do not create accounts. All accounts are created by parents or legal guardians. By creating a player profile for a child, you represent that you are that child's parent or legal guardian and that you consent to the collection and use of that child's information as described in this policy.

What we collect about children is limited to: name, date of birth, sport-specific profile information, and athletic performance data entered by the parent. Children's read-only access is provided through the Kids View feature only — children cannot edit data, create accounts, or communicate with others through the App.

Parents may at any time:

  • ◆Review the information collected about their child
  • ◆Request deletion of their child's data
  • ◆Revoke consent by deleting the player profile or closing their account

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@relnai.com.

Section 06

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information or your child's information to third parties.

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • ◆Service Providers. We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our backend database and authentication provider. Supabase processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • ◆Subscription Processing. Premium subscriptions are processed through RevenueCat and the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. These providers have their own privacy policies governing payment data, which we do not access or store.
  • ◆Legal Requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • ◆Business Transfers. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred. We will provide notice before any such transfer.
Section 07

Data Retention

We retain your account and player data for as long as your account is active. If you delete a player profile, all associated game data, stats, and achievement records are permanently deleted. If you delete your account, all data is permanently deleted. We may retain certain records for a limited period as required by law.

Section 08

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted authentication via Supabase Auth, row-level security policies on all database tables, and HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

Section 09

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@relnai.com. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell personal information.

Parents may review, correct, or request deletion of their child's information at any time by contacting privacy@relnai.com or by managing profiles directly within the App.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will provide notice through the App or by email. Your continued use of StatTap after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
